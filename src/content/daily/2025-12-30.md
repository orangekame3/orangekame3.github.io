I went back to my parents' home for the year-end holidays. This is my first visit since last New Year's. Since I had no particular plans, I spent time cleaning the house. On the way, I stopped at Odawara Station and bought a quantum computing book written by Prof. Fujii. I'm planning to read it on the Shinkansen on my way back home.
