- "parent's house" → "parents' house" (複数形の所有格。両親なので parents')
- "there is a Mount Kobo" → "there's a small hill called Mount Kobo" (「ちょっとした小山」= a small hill。Mount〜とだけ言うと大きな山のイメージ)
- "Since I have nothing to do" → "Since I had nothing particular to do" (過去形 + 「特に」= particular)
- "I climbed that" → "I went to climb it" (「登りにいった」= went to climb。行動の目的を表す)
- "I rethink" → "I was reminded" (「改めて〜思った」「しみじみ」= was reminded/realized anew)
- "my home country" → "my hometown" (「地元」= hometown, local area。countryだと国になる)
- "is so beautiful place" → "is such a beautiful place" (such a + 名詞 の形)
- "it is good to treck" → "I might go trekking" (「たまには〜行こうかな」= might go sometime; treckはtrekのスペルミス)

-->
