<!-- review:
- "In the end of year" → "At the end of the year" (「年末に」は "at the end of the year" が自然。"in" より "at" を使い、"the year" と定冠詞をつける)
- "preparing slide deck" → "preparing a slide deck" (不定冠詞 "a" が必要)
- "walking around parent's house" → "taking walks around my parents' house" (「散歩した」は "taking walks" がより自然。"parent's" → "parents'" で複数形の所有格に)
- "I ate a Motsu-nabe and Kamo-soba" → "I had motsu-nabe and kamo-soba" (食事には "had" がより自然。"a" は不要)
- "source of Aofojoayu and sesame source" → "the combination of Ajiaofujiao oil and sesame sauce" (「アオフォジャオユ」は「アジアオフージャオ油」と思われる。"source" → "sauce" のスペルミス。「組み合わせ」を明示的に)
- "is so much delicious" → "was incredibly delicious" (過去形に統一。"so much delicious" は文法的に不自然。"so delicious" または "incredibly delicious")
- "I will try it after going back my home" → "I'll try it again once I'm back home" (「帰省してから」は実家から自宅に戻ることを意味。"going back my home" → "back home" で自然に)

At the end of the year, I spent time preparing a slide deck and taking walks around my parents' house. In the evening, I had motsu-nabe and kamo-soba. To my surprise, the combination of Ajiaofujiao oil and sesame sauce was incredibly delicious. I'll try it again once I'm back home.
